# The 12 Jungian Brand Archetypes

Canonical framework from Margaret Mark & Carol Pearson's *The Hero and the Outlaw* (2001), adapted for brand strategy. Each archetype carries a distinct character that shapes palette, typography, tone, and voice.

Use these exact sigils, essences, and example brands in the archetype card deck (Phase 1 of the skill).

---

## ◈ Creator

- **Essence:** "Make something that didn't exist."
- **Drives:** Self-expression, originality, imagination
- **Voice:** Considered, generous, optimistic
- **Examples:** Apple · Lego · Adobe · Crayola · Etsy
- **Palette tendency:** Rich saturation, full spectrum, high-quality neutrals
- **Type tendency:** Distinctive display, human body type
- **Signature move:** A flourish that says "made, not assembled"

## ▲ Hero

- **Essence:** "Rise to the challenge, prove worth."
- **Drives:** Mastery, courage, achievement
- **Voice:** Direct, confident, action-oriented
- **Examples:** Nike · FedEx · Duracell · BMW · Gatorade
- **Palette tendency:** High contrast, bold accent, decisive
- **Type tendency:** Strong weight contrast, geometric display
- **Signature move:** A decisive edge — no apology in the silhouette

## ◯ Sage

- **Essence:** "Tell the truth, earn trust."
- **Drives:** Knowledge, understanding, expertise
- **Voice:** Clear, measured, authoritative without arrogance
- **Examples:** NYT · Google · BBC · Harvard · National Geographic
- **Palette tendency:** Low saturation, sophisticated neutrals, deep accents
- **Type tendency:** Editorial serif or workhorse humanist sans
- **Signature move:** Precision in spacing and hierarchy — nothing wasted

## ➤ Explorer

- **Essence:** "Keep moving, find the edge."
- **Drives:** Freedom, discovery, authenticity
- **Voice:** Curious, undomesticated, grounded
- **Examples:** REI · Jeep · North Face · Patagonia · Airbnb
- **Palette tendency:** Earth tones, muted natural colors, occasional vivid accent
- **Type tendency:** Confident humanist, slightly rough edges
- **Signature move:** Textural detail — something that's been somewhere

## ✕ Outlaw

- **Essence:** "Break what should be broken."
- **Drives:** Liberation, disruption, revolution
- **Voice:** Blunt, irreverent, uncompromising
- **Examples:** Harley-Davidson · Virgin · Diesel · MINI · Vans
- **Palette tendency:** Desaturated + high contrast, black/red, anti-trend
- **Type tendency:** Brutalist, stenciled, or extreme-condensed
- **Signature move:** A refusal — a deliberate break from polish

## ✦ Magician

- **Essence:** "Transform what is into what could be."
- **Drives:** Vision, transformation, possibility
- **Voice:** Wonder-inducing, aspirational, precise
- **Examples:** Disney · Tesla · Dyson · MasterCard · Polaroid
- **Palette tendency:** Deep jewel tones with luminous accents, night/starlight pairings
- **Type tendency:** Elegant modern serif or precise geometric
- **Signature move:** An optical illusion or paradox — something that shouldn't work

## ☺ Jester

- **Essence:** "Lightness is a form of truth."
- **Drives:** Joy, play, connection
- **Voice:** Warm, irreverent-with-care, surprising
- **Examples:** MailChimp · Old Spice · Ben & Jerry's · Doritos · Slack
- **Palette tendency:** Vivid, slightly unexpected pairings, optimistic
- **Type tendency:** Rounded sans, hand-drawn accents
- **Signature move:** A wink — something slightly off-kilter in the frame

## ❖ Ruler

- **Essence:** "Set the standard. Hold the frame."
- **Drives:** Order, control, legacy
- **Voice:** Composed, certain, declarative
- **Examples:** Rolex · Mercedes · American Express · Louis Vuitton · Microsoft
- **Palette tendency:** Deep neutrals + metallic accent, restrained luxury
- **Type tendency:** Classical serif, refined geometric
- **Signature move:** Absolute symmetry and negative space as power

## ♥ Caregiver

- **Essence:** "Take care of what matters."
- **Drives:** Nurture, protection, compassion
- **Voice:** Warm, steady, unhurried
- **Examples:** Johnson's · Volvo · TOMS · UNICEF · Campbell's
- **Palette tendency:** Soft warm neutrals, dusty accents, no hard edges
- **Type tendency:** Rounded humanist, soft terminals
- **Signature move:** Generous whitespace — room to breathe

## ❋ Lover

- **Essence:** "Make it beautiful. Make them feel."
- **Drives:** Intimacy, beauty, pleasure
- **Voice:** Sensual, attentive, inviting
- **Examples:** Chanel · Häagen-Dazs · Godiva · Victoria's Secret · Tiffany
- **Palette tendency:** Jewel tones, rich warm neutrals, elegant pairings
- **Type tendency:** Italic-forward, expressive serif, elegant contrast
- **Signature move:** A caress in the detail — curves that linger

## ○ Innocent

- **Essence:** "See it clean. Start fresh."
- **Drives:** Simplicity, optimism, purity
- **Voice:** Direct, honest, unguarded
- **Examples:** Dove · Coca-Cola · Innocent Drinks · Aveeno · Method
- **Palette tendency:** Soft pastels or crisp primaries, generous white
- **Type tendency:** Rounded sans, clean humanist
- **Signature move:** Obvious simplicity that took effort to achieve

## ▢ Everyman

- **Essence:** "Welcome as you are."
- **Drives:** Belonging, reliability, common ground
- **Voice:** Grounded, unpretentious, direct
- **Examples:** Target · IKEA · Home Depot · Costco · Levi's
- **Palette tendency:** Dependable primaries, grounded neutrals, practical
- **Type tendency:** Workhorse sans, readable at all sizes
- **Signature move:** Obvious utility — nothing to decode

## Primary + Secondary Pairings

Standard practice: one archetype drives 70% of the character, another drives 30%. The combination produces a distinctive tension that a single archetype can't capture alone.

Common generative pairs:

- **Creator + Magician** — ambitious aesthetic, wonder-making (Apple in its "think different" era)
- **Sage + Hero** — authoritative competence (Google, McKinsey)
- **Hero + Outlaw** — disruptive performance (Tesla when combative)
- **Caregiver + Sage** — trusted warmth (Mayo Clinic, Volvo)
- **Lover + Ruler** — luxury (Chanel, Rolex)
- **Explorer + Creator** — bold originality (Patagonia, Airbnb)
- **Jester + Everyman** — accessible delight (IKEA, Slack)
- **Innocent + Caregiver** — gentle care (Dove, Method)

These pairings are examples — accept any user combination. Mark primary and secondary in the stored state.
